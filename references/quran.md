# Quran and religious-content reference

This reference applies only when the subject needs it. The main skill is a general explainer-video workflow.

Verify verse numbers, Arabic text, translation wording and attributed observations against the actual Quran passage and a reliable source. Read the surrounding context. Don't turn a usage pattern into an absolute dictionary rule; the rain example explicitly accounts for Quran 4:102 rather than claiming *matar* always means punishment.

Distinguish the Arabic quotation, its English rendering and the narrator's explanation. If showing a partial verse, identify the reference and avoid implying that the excerpt is the entire ayah. Preserve Arabic letters and vowel marks through display and speech input. Give the Arabic enough space; inspect marks above/below the letters at final resolution.

When requested, speak the ayah in Arabic and then give its English translation. Plan additional time for both. Use a verified translation within its attribution/reuse requirements or clearly identify a concise original rendering; don't invent precise quotations. Retain sources in the project.

For the supplied example style, use generic modern readers and teaching props rather than representing Allah, prophets or named historical people. A telescope, calendar or sealed letter is a metaphor; don't present it as a literal picture of unseen realities. Adapt to the user's requirements and discuss genuinely material imagery choices before production if unclear.

In the adraka/yudrika example the careful claim is that the specific answer isn't supplied there. Avoid changing it into "humans can never learn this" or "nothing about the subject is explained." Its `SOURCES.md` identifies the four passages and the attribution to Sufyan ibn Uyaynah.

Script/source checks, automated Arabic transcription, and human recitation review are different levels of evidence. Report what was actually done. Example source notes are evidence for those example projects; they are not a substitute for verifying a new topic.
