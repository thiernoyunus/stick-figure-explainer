# Original action study B

Complete source study of **all 16 registered scenes** in the three assigned files: 14 in `scenes-p4.js`, one in `diffity-loop.js`, one in `diffity-agents.js`. This is the B contribution to the larger atlas, not a claim to have inspected all 32 scenes visually. Four detailed sheets are linked below; the other twelve entries are source observations.

Paths resolve from this document. `#L…` identifies the original source line. Timing is seconds into an action unless stated otherwise. A scene's ending often restores its website idle; a Reel should hold the useful result and cut before that reset. Do not copy product names or software labels into an unrelated story.

## Three guidance changes to carry forward

1. **Choose a physical verb that explains the sentence.** Reach → grip → move → release → result gives more variety than repeating a walk and a click. Borrow the throw from `p:mongodumper`, catch-and-tip from `p:local-ses`, peel from `p:copy-dom-css`, feed from `p:timelang`, fit from `p:claude-statusline`, and crank from `p:balsamiq-sans-neue` only when the thought needs that action.
2. **Make weight, contact and delayed response visible.** Keep a prop at the actual hand until release; follow the moving grip with the body. Add a small wind-up, follow-through, impact wobble or trailing sway from that same scene. For two characters, give each a separate task and reaction; `diffity-loop` includes two real 1.2-second thinking holds. More characters alone do not establish friendship or shared carrying.
3. **End on the changed object, timed to the spoken answer.** The box reaches the bucket, the caught letter appears in the inbox, three fixes become visible, or one selected robot lights up. Secondary motion supports this cause and result. Do not fill every sentence with cheering, sparkles or an early answer reveal.
